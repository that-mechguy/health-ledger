Health Ledger · Privacy policy

Health Ledger is a personal health dashboard used by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads. With your permission, it reads your own activity, heart, sleep and body measurements from the Google Health API (read-only), plus data you send from Apple Health and the reports you upload.

Where it is stored. In the owner's private Supabase database, protected by login and row-level security. Google access tokens are stored server-side and are never shown in the website.

Sharing. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. It is used only to show the dashboard to the signed-in owner.

Removing access. Use “Disconnect” in the dashboard's Settings, or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
